Quick answers

How long does shipping take?

Shipping typically takes 4–6 weeks from Minneapolis to Lomé, depending on the shipping schedule.

How do I pay?

We confirm the payment options with your quote, so you know exactly what to expect before anything ships.

How do I track my package?

Use the tracking number we give you after registration on our Track page, or sign in to your account.
