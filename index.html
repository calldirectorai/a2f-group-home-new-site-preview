FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Estimated shipping time from Minneapolis to Lomé is typically 4–6 weeks. Actual timing can vary based on shipment schedules, cargo handling, customs clearance, and final delivery arrangements.

Can I send multiple packages?

Yes. A2F can help consolidate multiple eligible packages into one shipment.

Can A2F purchase an item for me?

Yes. Use the Shop & Ship request form to send a product link or item request. A2F will confirm availability and pricing before purchasing.

How do I request a quote?

Use the shipping estimate form, contact the team by phone or email, or start a WhatsApp conversation.

Can I track my shipment?

A2F provides shipment-status updates. Tracking options may depend on the service selected and shipment stage.

Are all items eligible for shipment?

Some items may be restricted or require special handling. Contact A2F before sending items that may be regulated, hazardous, fragile, or subject to customs restrictions.

Do I need to pay customs duties?

Customs requirements and any applicable fees may vary by item and shipment. A2F can provide guidance based on your shipment details.
